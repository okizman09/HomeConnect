# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## General
**Q: How do I reset my password?**
A: Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password".

**Q: Is my personal data safe?**
A: Yes, we use encryption and follow strict privacy policies.

## For Tenants
**Q: Can I pay rent in installments?**
A: This depends on the specific agreement with your Landlord. Check your lease.

**Q: What if the landlord refuses to fix a major issue?**
A: You can report the issue to the Housing Authority after documenting the refusal.

## For Landlords
**Q: How do I verify a tenant?**
A: We act as a connection platform. We recommend requesting ID and proof of income independently.

**Q: Can I edit a listing after posting?**
A: Yes, go to "My Listings" and click the edit icon.

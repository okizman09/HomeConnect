# HomeConnect Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026-01-12

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Account Information**: Name, email, phone number.
- **Lease Documents**: Lease agreements uploaded for analysis.
- **Maintenance Data**: Descriptions and images of maintenance issues.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
- To facilitate landlord-tenant communication.
- To analyze lease documents using AI (Gemini) to provide summaries and insights.
- To classify and prioritize maintenance requests.

## 3. AI Data Processing
We use Google's Gemini API to process certain data.
- **Lease Analysis**: Uploaded leases are processed to extract key terms.
- **Maintenance**: Descriptions are analyzed to determine urgency and category.
Data sent to the AI API is used solely for generating responses and is not retained by the AI provider for model training, in accordance with their API data policies.

## 4. Data Security
We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data.

## 5. Contact
For privacy concerns, contact privacy@homeconnect.com.

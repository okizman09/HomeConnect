# HomeConnect Tenant Handbook

## Maintenance Guidelines
- **Emergency**: Gas leaks, major water leaks, total power loss. Report immediately. 
  - Action: Turn off supply if possible. Call emergency services if life-threatening.
- **High Priority**: AC failure in summer, refrigerator failure, mold.
  - Action: Report via app. Expect response within 24 hours.
- **Normal Priority**: Dripping tap, minor cosmetic issues.
  - Action: Report via app. Expect response within 3-5 days.

## Rent Payments
- Rent is due on the 1st of every month.
- Late fees apply after the 5th (5% of monthly rent).
- Payments can be made via bank transfer or in-app payment (coming soon).

## Noise Policy
- Quiet hours are from 10 PM to 7 AM.
- Repeated noise complaints may lead to lease termination.

## Guest Policy
- Guests staying more than 3 consecutive nights require landlord approval.
